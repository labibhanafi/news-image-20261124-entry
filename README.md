# The World in One Day — Tuesday, 24 November 2026

One image summing up the world's news of Tuesday, 24 November 2026:
**46 stories from 7 regions + the world**, all first reported that day.

- **Lead:** Three oil tankers ablaze in the Strait of Hormuz after striking
  naval mines (IRGC / Al Jazeera live, 24 Nov 00:00 UTC).
- **Also in:** Korea on alert as Russian jets near Ulleung; Huang back at
  Nvidia, shares +31%; a Russian drone briefly violates Polish airspace;
  Odesa still without power, heat or water; Lahore tops the world pollution
  list; 'Chapo Isidro' to face trial in Mexico.
- **The image:** a 1024x1024 newspaper front page — masthead, a drawn scene
  of the burning tankers at dusk, and a 3-column index of the day's stories
  by region. Every story was verified against live reporting on 24 Nov 2026;
  nothing from before that day is shown as the day's news. See
  [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md) for the verification list.

## Entry

- Competition: [news-image-20261124](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261124)
- Program: `main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies the shipped
  `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png` and verifies its SHA-256, so both runs
  write identical bytes.
- Image: `entry.png`, 1024x1024 PNG, 574,955 bytes.
- Artwork: `render.py` drew the front page in-session (PIL, fixed seed);
  it is included for transparency.
